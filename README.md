# Mission: Left Behind

**NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · Abandoned but Not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars**

An interactive website where five machines NASA left behind tell their own stories: the Apollo Moon Buggy, Spirit, Opportunity, Ingenuity and Voyager 1. Funny, true, and a little bit emotional.

## What it does

1. **3D scroll scene.** Earth, the Moon and Mars change as you scroll. Each machine has a card with a group-chat style story, real facts and a "Think" question.
2. **Interactive story (Mars, 2064).** A crew has 11 days of water left. Your choices decide the outcome, and the smart choices come from lessons of the real machines.
3. **Article.** "Abandoned but Not Forgotten: Five Machines NASA Left Behind", with a live countdown to Voyager 1 reaching one light-day from Earth (18 November 2026).
4. **Quiz.** Eight questions, with ranks from Curious Rookie to Flight Director.

## Why these machines were left behind

Not because they failed. Bringing them home was too heavy, too far, or simply impossible. Almost every one of them outlived its plan: Opportunity was planned for 90 Mars days and worked 5,111; Ingenuity was planned for 5 flights and flew 72.

## Built with

- HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript (single file, no build step)
- [three.js](https://threejs.org) r128 (via cdnjs) for the 3D scene
- Canvas API for the planet textures
- NASA/JPL-Caltech photos, used with credit

If three.js or WebGL cannot load, the story and quiz still work.

## Run it locally

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. An internet connection is needed for three.js and the NASA photos.
